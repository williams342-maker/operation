## Remaining Manual Items

- **Signature capture:** The Attorney Sign-off block (final page) is intentionally blank and awaits counsel's printed name, firm, bar ID/jurisdiction, wet-ink/DocuSign signature, and date.
- **TOC refresh (DOCX only):** When editing the DOCX, users must press F9 (or right-click → Update Field) in Word to refresh TOC entries after headings change. The `w:updateFields="true"` setting in `settings.xml` also triggers Word to prompt for update on first open. The distribution PDF ships with the TOC already populated.
- **Google Ads Conversion Labels:** Unrelated to the binder — three real Google Ads Conversion Labels remain outstanding for marketplace ad-events wiring (Phase D operational dependency, blocked on user provision).
