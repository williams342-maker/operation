# Quarterly Legal Review Cadence

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Internal reference. Not published.
**Owner:** Operations Lead + Legal Lead
**Last updated:** 2026-06-30

---

## Purpose

Keep the Crafters Market Trust & Policy Center current, defensible, and consistent as the marketplace evolves. This cadence exists so counsel review is not a one-shot at launch — it becomes an operational rhythm that catches drift before it becomes exposure.

---

## The Three Triggers for Legal Review

### 1. Standing Quarterly Review

- **Frequency:** Once per calendar quarter (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4).
- **Target duration:** 30–60 minutes of counsel time per quarter.
- **Format:** Counsel receives a **Quarterly Review Packet** (template below) and returns either "no changes needed" or a short list of edits.
- **Owner:** Operations Lead prepares the packet. Legal Lead schedules with counsel.
- **Outputs:** Counsel sign-off recorded in this cadence log (below). Any tracked edits applied within 30 days of receipt.

### 2. Post-Material-Feature Review

Triggered immediately after **any material product launch** that touches:

- Payments, payouts, fees, or commissions
- Buyer Protection or Marketplace Assistance mechanics
- Any new user data collection
- Any new integration that receives Buyer or Maker data (Stripe, ad platforms, analytics vendors, email/SMS providers)
- Onboarding flows (Maker application, Buyer registration)
- Content policies (Prohibited Items, Community Guidelines)
- International shipping or cross-border payments
- Subscription pricing (Crafters Plus tiers, promotional pricing)

**Format:** Ad-hoc scope — counsel reviews only the affected policy sections.

### 3. Post-Regulatory-Change Review

Triggered immediately when a **material regulatory change** affects our operations:

- State privacy law updates (CCPA/CPRA amendments, new state privacy statutes)
- FTC guidance updates (endorsement/review rules, "Made in USA" claims, subscription auto-renewal)
- Marketplace-facilitator sales tax law changes (any new state)
- EU/UK cross-border requirements if we open internationally (GDPR, UK IDTA, DSA)
- Payment regulations (Stripe policy changes with pass-through terms, PCI updates)
- IP / copyright statutes (DMCA amendments, state-level rights)

**Owner:** Legal Lead monitors industry updates and triggers review.

## Quarterly Review Packet Template

Send this to counsel at the start of each quarter.

```
Subject: Crafters Market — Quarterly Legal Review · Q[N] 20[YY]

Attached:
  1. Change log since last review (diff summary)
  2. Traffic & activation snapshot (Buyers, Makers, Orders in the quarter)
  3. Support ticket themes (top 5 policy-adjacent complaints)
  4. Chargeback / dispute summary (count, categories, outcomes)
  5. Any new integrations added
  6. Any regulatory news items we're tracking

Please confirm whether any of the twelve Trust & Policy Center documents
require changes based on the above. Priority focus areas per our launch
cover sheet:

  (1) Consistency across policies
  (2) Marketplace-facilitator responsibilities
  (3) Washington State considerations
  (4) Privacy disclosures
  (5) Payment / fee language
  (6) Seller obligations
  (7) Dispute resolution
  (8) Required consumer disclosures
  (9) Any Appendix A items that need reopening

Target turnaround: 5 business days.
```

## Change Log Preparation Checklist

Before sending each quarterly packet, the Operations Lead must confirm:

- ✅ CHANGELOG.md updated with everything shipped since last review
- ✅ ROADMAP.md updated with anything material queued
- ✅ Any new integrations documented in the Privacy Policy vendor list
- ✅ Any new cookie/pixel additions documented in the Cookie Policy
- ✅ Any commission or fee changes reflected in the Fee & Pricing Policy
- ✅ Any policy version bumps recorded in the manifest revision_history

## Not-A-Substitute Disclaimer

This cadence is an **operational rhythm**, not a substitute for competent legal counsel on any specific issue. Anything that materially affects marketplace liability, consumer rights, or regulatory posture should go to counsel immediately, not wait for the quarterly window.
