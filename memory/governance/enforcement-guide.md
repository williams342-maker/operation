# Enforcement Guide (Internal)

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Internal reference. Not published.
**Owner:** Trust & Safety Lead
**Last updated:** 2026-06-30

---

## Purpose

Standardizes how Crafters Market enforces its marketplace policies. Every enforcement decision must map to a step on this ladder and reference the underlying policy section.

---

## 1. Enforcement Ladder

### Step 0 — Coaching (not enforcement)

- **When:** Quality issues that are not policy violations (blurry photos, missing Shop Policies, unclear processing times).
- **What:** Send a coaching message with specific recommendations. Offer resources (Photography Guide, Shop Policies Templates when available).
- **Documentation:** Log the coaching contact in the Maker's account notes.

### Step 1 — Warning

- **When:** First-time minor violation (e.g., missing allergen disclosure on a cottage-food Listing; using a stock photo the Maker owns but hasn't disclosed).
- **What:** Formal warning sent via account notice + email. Listing may be edited or temporarily paused pending correction.
- **Documentation:** Enforcement record with policy citation. Warning counts toward strike history.
- **Appeal:** Same-day informal appeal via support email; formal Appeals Process available.

### Step 2 — Listing Removal

- **When:** Clear policy violation on a specific Listing (Prohibited Items, IP infringement, undisclosed drop-shipping).
- **What:** Listing removed from public view. Maker notified with policy citation and appeal path. Any open Orders on that Listing are fulfilled normally unless the violation implicates the Order itself.
- **Documentation:** Enforcement record with policy citation, screenshots, and reviewer identity.

### Step 3 — Temporary Suspension

- **When:** Repeat violations after warning; single serious violation (e.g., harassment of a Buyer, review manipulation); pending investigation of alleged fraud.
- **Duration options:** 7 days · 14 days · 30 days.
- **What:** Account cannot list new items or accept new Orders. Existing Orders are either allowed to fulfill (with Buyer notification) or refunded via Marketplace Assistance depending on severity. Payouts may be held.
- **Documentation:** Full record. Escalation to Trust & Safety Lead required.
- **Appeal:** Formal Appeals Process.

### Step 4 — Permanent Removal

- **When:** Egregious violation (verified fraud, sale of prohibited weapons/drugs, verified CSAM, repeat IP infringement crossing repeat-infringer threshold), or third suspension.
- **What:** Account permanently closed. Listings removed. Payout balance released only after all open Order disputes are resolved. Any recoverable amounts (chargebacks, marketplace-funded refunds, unpaid fees) are set off against the balance.
- **Documentation:** Full record with Founder sign-off. Legal Lead notified if litigation or law-enforcement referral is contemplated.
- **Appeal:** Formal Appeals Process. Decisions on permanent removal are reviewed by Founder.

---

## 2. Aggravating Factors

Move up the ladder when:

- Prior enforcement actions exist on the account.
- Violation involves safety, IP, or fraud (as opposed to quality/consistency).
- Violation is systematic (many Listings, not one).
- Maker was previously coached on the same issue.
- Maker misrepresented facts during initial investigation.

## 3. Mitigating Factors

Move down the ladder when:

- No prior enforcement actions.
- Maker took prompt corrective action.
- Violation appears inadvertent (e.g., unfamiliar rule) and the Maker is willing to comply.
- Underlying policy was recently changed.

---

## 4. Evidence Standards

Every enforcement action must include:

1. Policy section link (e.g., `/policies/prohibited-items#toc-4`).
2. Screenshot or archived copy of the offending content.
3. Any Buyer or Maker messages establishing intent/context.
4. Reviewer name, timestamp, decision rationale.
5. Action taken.

Retention: **3 years** minimum, or as required by law.

---

## 5. Appeals Process

- Every enforcement action includes an appeal path.
- **First-tier appeal:** informal reply to the enforcement email; reviewed by a different moderator within 5 business days.
- **Second-tier appeal:** Trust & Safety Lead review within 10 business days.
- **Third-tier appeal (permanent removals only):** Founder review within 20 business days.
- Appeals reference the specific policy section, the evidence originally cited, and the Maker's counter-evidence.

Appeals do not automatically stay enforcement, except for permanent removals which pause while the third-tier appeal is pending unless there is ongoing harm.

---

## 6. Special Situations

### 6.1 Chargebacks

- Chargebacks initiated by Buyers are handled through Stripe.
- If the chargeback is upheld, the amount is deducted from the Maker's balance.
- Repeated chargebacks (chargeback rate > 1% of transactions or > 3 chargebacks in 90 days) trigger a Manual Review.

### 6.2 DMCA / IP claims

- Notice: designated agent processes per 17 U.S.C. § 512.
- Repeat-infringer threshold: **3 substantiated notices in 12 months** → permanent removal.
- Counter-notice honored per statute (10–14 business days).

### 6.3 Safety incidents

- Reports of injury or safety incidents move directly to Manual Review regardless of prior history.
- Listing pulled pending investigation.
- Notify Legal Lead and Founder.

### 6.4 Law enforcement requests

- All requests routed to Legal Lead.
- Do not respond to non-legal-process requests. Do not disclose subscriber records without a lawful order or consent.

---

## 7. Confidentiality

- Reporters remain confidential unless required by law.
- Do not name reporters in communications with the reported Maker.
- Enforcement decisions are not publicly discussed except in aggregated transparency reports.
