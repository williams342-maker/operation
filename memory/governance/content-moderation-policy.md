# Content Moderation Policy (Internal)

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Internal reference. Not published.
**Owner:** Trust & Safety Lead
**Last updated:** 2026-06-30

---

## Purpose

Defines how the moderation team enforces the **Prohibited Items Policy**, **Community Guidelines**, **Intellectual Property & DMCA Policy**, and **Maker Agreement** on Crafters Market.

This document is for internal reference. Users see the public policies; the moderation team uses this document to apply them consistently.

---

## 1. Scope

Content moderation applies to:

- Listings (title, description, photos, tags, variant metadata)
- User Content (journals, showcase submissions, reviews)
- Messages between Buyers and Makers
- Maker Shop pages (banner, about text, Shop Policies)
- Community posts

---

## 2. Triage Categories

Every moderation item is triaged into one of four categories:

| Category | Definition | SLA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P0 — Emergency** | Illegal content, imminent safety risk, active fraud, credible IP takedown with irreparable harm claim. | Same-business-day action. Immediate removal + Maker notified. |
| **P1 — Policy violation, clear** | Unambiguous violation of Prohibited Items, Community Guidelines, or IP policy. | 2 business days. Standard enforcement path. |
| **P2 — Policy violation, borderline** | Requires manual review, context, or Maker input before action. | 5 business days. Escalate to Trust & Safety Lead if needed. |
| **P3 — Quality/consistency** | Not a violation but below marketplace quality bar (image quality, unclear descriptions, missing Shop Policies). | 10 business days. Coaching, not enforcement. |

---

## 3. Sources of Moderation Items

- **User reports** — Report button on Listings, messages, and reviews. Highest-priority intake path.
- **Automated flags** — Keyword filters, image similarity checks, price anomaly flags.
- **Manual sweeps** — Weekly review of new Listings from new Makers.
- **IP claims** — DMCA/trademark takedown notices routed to the designated agent.
- **Support tickets** — Support triage escalations.

---

## 4. Standard Enforcement Actions

See `enforcement-guide.md` for the full ladder. Short version:

1. **Warning** — First-time minor violation. Listing may be edited/removed. Maker notified.
2. **Listing Removal** — Non-compliant Listing removed. Maker notified with policy citation.
3. **Temporary Suspension** — 7 / 14 / 30 days depending on severity. Payouts held for open Orders.
4. **Permanent Removal** — Repeat violations, egregious violations, or verified fraud.
5. **Appeal** — Every enforcement action offers an appeal path (see Appeals Process).

---

## 5. Evidence Standards

Every enforcement action must be documented with:

- The specific policy section violated (link to `/policies/:slug#toc-N`)
- Screenshots or archived copies of the offending content
- Any Maker/Buyer messages that establish intent or context
- The reviewer's name, timestamp, and decision rationale
- The action taken (warning / removal / suspension / permanent)

Documentation is retained for **at least 3 years** or as long as required by applicable law.

---

## 6. Bias & Consistency Checks

- Trust & Safety Lead conducts a **monthly bias audit** — random sample of 20 recent enforcement actions reviewed for consistency.
- Any pattern suggesting inconsistent enforcement (by Maker category, geography, or account age) is escalated to Founder for policy or training adjustment.

---

## 7. Handling of Protected Speech

Reviews and journal posts are subject to a higher bar for removal than Listings. Do not remove reviews or journals solely because a Maker requests it. Legitimate grounds for review removal:

- Contains PII of another User (address, phone, real name of a private individual).
- Contains threats, harassment, or discrimination against protected classes.
- Constitutes review manipulation (paid, coerced, or exchanged reviews).
- Falsifies factual claims that could harm the Platform's or another Maker's reputation.
- Violates the Community Guidelines in another concrete way.

**Not grounds for removal:** the reviewer's opinion is negative or the Maker disputes the reviewer's characterization.

---

## 8. Handling of IP Claims

- All DMCA notices routed to the designated agent (see IP & DMCA Policy).
- Counter-notice window: 10–14 business days per 17 U.S.C. § 512.
- Repeat-infringer accounts (3+ substantiated notices) permanently removed.
- Trademark claims handled by parallel-but-distinct process (DMCA covers copyright only).

---

## 9. Confidentiality

- Reporter identities are kept confidential unless disclosure is legally required.
- Enforcement decisions are not publicly announced (except in rare cases involving marketplace-wide safety).
- Communication with the affected Maker is factual, cites the policy, and offers the appeal path.
