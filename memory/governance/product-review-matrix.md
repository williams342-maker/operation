# Product Review Matrix (Internal)

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Internal reference. Not published.
**Owner:** Trust & Safety Lead
**Last updated:** 2026-06-30

---

## Purpose

Every Listing on Crafters Market falls into one of **four review dispositions**. This document defines those dispositions and lists common item categories with their default disposition.

Public-facing text lives in the **Prohibited Items Policy** (`/policies/prohibited-items`). This matrix is the operational lookup used by the moderation team.

---

## Dispositions

| Disposition | Meaning | Reviewer Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Allowed** | Fits Crafters Market's curated-marketplace scope. No conditions. | Approve. |
| **Allowed with Conditions** | Fits, but Listing must include specific disclosures, warnings, materials, or documentation. | Approve if conditions are met. Otherwise return to Maker with guidance. |
| **Manual Review** | Cannot be auto-approved. Requires human review for context, materials, safety, or IP. | Escalate to Trust & Safety. Approve/deny with documented rationale. |
| **Prohibited** | Cannot be sold on Crafters Market. | Remove Listing. Warn or suspend Maker per enforcement guide. |

## Category Defaults

The list below is illustrative. When a new category emerges, the Trust & Safety Lead adds it here with the appropriate disposition.

### Allowed

- Handmade home goods (candles, ceramics, textiles, wall art)
- Original handmade jewelry (metals, resin, wood, gemstones)
- Original handmade apparel and accessories (bags, hats, scarves)
- Original handmade paper goods (cards, journals, prints from original art)
- Original handmade woodworking (cutting boards, small furniture, decor)
- Original handmade craft supplies (yarn, buttons, findings — hand-produced)
- Digital patterns, SVGs, printables designed by the Maker
- Custom Orders where the Maker personalizes original work

### Allowed with Conditions

- **Cosmetics / bath & body** — must include ingredient list; must be manufactured in a compliant facility; no unapproved therapeutic claims.
- **Consumables (baked goods, jams, etc.)** — allowed **only** if the Maker's jurisdiction permits cottage-food sales and the Maker complies with local labeling. Listing must include allergen disclosures.
- **Items with small parts** — Listings for items with small parts marketed to any age group must include the standard choking-hazard disclosure.
- **Vintage / pre-owned** — the Listing must clearly state "Vintage" or "Pre-owned" and describe condition honestly. **No resale of new mass-produced goods.**
- **AI-assisted design** — Listings that use AI tools in the design workflow must disclose the extent of AI involvement per the Prohibited Items Policy. AI-only Listings (no substantial human craft) are Prohibited.
- **Fan art / licensed characters** — allowed only if the Maker owns or has licensed the underlying IP, or the use is a clear parody/transformative use. Manual review recommended.
- **Herbal / essential-oil products** — allowed with ingredient labels; no therapeutic claims; no unregistered essential-oil blends marketed as remedies.

### Manual Review

- Items with public-safety implications (children's toys, infant products, safety equipment)
- Items using restricted materials (ivory-look substitutes, exotic wood, feathers, certain animal products)
- Items making health, medical, or safety claims of any kind
- Items using recognizable brand names, logos, characters, or team marks
- Items marketed as replicas of copyrighted or trademarked works
- Large-volume Listings from new Makers (100+ units of the same SKU on a new account) — resale/dropship suspicion
- Any Listing where category classification is ambiguous

### Prohibited

- Mass-produced or wholesale goods where the Maker is not the maker
- Drop-shipped items
- Counterfeit or infringing items (unauthorized use of trademarks, logos, characters)
- Weapons: firearms, ammunition, functional edged weapons designed to injure people, tasers/stun guns where prohibited
- Drugs (recreational, prescription, or unregistered controlled substances) and drug paraphernalia
- Regulated substances requiring licenses the Maker does not hold (alcohol, cannabis/CBD in restricted jurisdictions, nicotine products)
- Live animals or animal products from endangered species
- Recalled products (including recalled children's products)
- Adult / sexually explicit content
- Hate speech, symbols promoting hate against protected classes, or Nazi/paramilitary paraphernalia
- Human remains (except cleaned/preserved specimens where lawful and disclosed)
- Items that violate any applicable law in the Maker's or Buyer's jurisdiction
- AI-only Listings where no substantial human craft is involved
- Health products making unapproved therapeutic claims

## Escalation Ladder for Ambiguous Items

1. Assigned moderator makes an initial call with rationale.
2. If disposition is Manual Review, ticket routed to Trust & Safety Lead.
3. If Trust & Safety Lead is unsure, escalate to Founder with recommendation.
4. Founder decision becomes the new precedent — matrix updated.
