# Maker email — "Light theme rollout"

Subject lines (pick one):
- A new look on Crafters Market — easier on the eyes
- We brightened things up. Here's what changed.
- Light mode is here (and you can still go dark)

---

Hey {{maker_first_name}},

Quick heads-up: you'll notice **Crafters Market looks different** the next
time you log in.

We've heard from several of you — and a growing number of buyers — that the
old dark theme was tough to read for long stretches. Studio managers
scrolling through orders, buyers reading product descriptions on bright
phone screens at the farmer's market, makers reviewing payouts on a porch
in afternoon sun — the contrast just wasn't holding up.

So we changed it.

### What's new

- **Light cream paper background** as the new default — easier on the eyes,
  no more squinting at gray-on-black text
- **Bigger, bolder type** for headlines and product titles
- **Orange accents stay** — the brand color you know is still front and
  center, just used more intentionally
- **Dark mode is still available** — there's a sun/moon toggle in the top
  navigation if you want it back. We'll remember your choice on every
  device you sign in from.
- The entire **maker dashboard** got the same treatment: sidebar, listings,
  orders, payouts, marketing tools, settings — everything is now legible
  in any lighting

### Anything I need to do?

Nothing. Your products, orders, payouts, messages, and settings are exactly
where you left them. Just sign in and the new look loads automatically.

### Don't love it?

Click the **moon icon** in the top right and you're back to the dark
workshop-after-hours look. Your choice is saved per-browser.

We listened. We shipped. If anything still feels off, hit reply and tell
me — I read every message.

— The Crafters Market team
{{cta_button_label: Visit the new dashboard → https://craftersmarket.org/maker/dashboard}}

*PS: We also rotated the homepage — buyers landing on craftersmarket.org
now see four different craft-themed hero scenes (woodworking, leather,
