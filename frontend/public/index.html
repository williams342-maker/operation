Precision CNC Art & Handcrafted Goods, Built By Vetted Independent Makers.

Crafters Market is a curated online marketplace for handmade CNC metal art, plasma-cut steel signs, custom wood signs, monogram decor, engraved cutting boards, and made-to-order originals. Every piece is built by an approved independent artisan working out of their own shop or garage — never mass-produced, never drop-shipped.

Shop one-of-a-kind CNC metalwork and woodwork, commission a fully custom build, or discover makers in your state. Checkout is secured by Stripe, shipping is nationwide across the United States, and every order pays the maker directly.

What you can buy on Crafters Market

  • Plasma-cut metal signs — family names, ranch brands, business logos, veteran & first-responder tributes.
  • CNC wood signs & monograms — wedding decor, nursery signs, housewarming gifts, bar & garage signage.
  • Custom cutting boards & charcuterie — engraved hardwood, end-grain and edge-grain builds.
  • Made-to-order originals — send your own art, photo, or idea and commission a one-off piece from a verified maker.
  • Downloadable CNC & laser files — SVG, DXF, F3D and STL patterns from professional pattern designers.

Why Crafters Market is different

Unlike generic handmade marketplaces, every maker on Crafters Market is individually reviewed before listing a single product. We verify real workshop photos, real machines, and real past work — so what you buy is actually hand-built in the United States, not rebranded overseas imports.

Makers keep the majority of every sale. Buyers get direct messaging with the artisan, realistic lead times, and the option to request revisions before the piece ships.
