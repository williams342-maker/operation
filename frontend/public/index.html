Why Crafters Market is different

Unlike generic handmade marketplaces, every maker on Crafters Market is individually vetted before listing a single product. We verify real workshop photos, real machines, and real past work — so what you buy is actually handcrafted and built in the United States, not rebranded overseas imports.

Vetted makers keep the majority of every sale. Buyers get direct messaging with the artisan, realistic lead times, and the option to request revisions before the precision-built piece ships.

How a Crafters Market order works

  1. Find your piece. Browse handcrafted wall art, custom signs, address numbers, business signage, and made-to-order CNC pieces. Filter by technique (plasma, laser, router), category, or price. Save favorites to revisit later, or follow a maker to get notified when they list new work.
  2. Talk to the maker. Want a custom size, a different finish, or your family name on it? Message the maker directly from the listing page. Most reply within twenty-four hours and can quote a custom build in a single thread. The same artisan who'll cut your piece is the one answering your questions — there's no support layer between you and the workshop.
  3. Pay safely. Checkout uses Stripe — the same processor Shopify, Substack, and tens of thousands of indie storefronts trust. Every transaction is encrypted, your card is never stored on our servers, and Apple Pay, Google Pay, and standard card payments are all supported. If your order ever runs into trouble, Stripe's buyer protection covers you on every purchase.
  4. Track to your door. The moment your maker drops off the package, tracking auto-fires to your inbox and (if you opt in) as a browser push notification. We never spam — just shipping updates, a delivery confirmation, and a one-tap link to leave the maker a review afterward. The whole flow is designed to feel like buying from a friend who runs a small workshop, except backed by real fulfillment infrastructure.
